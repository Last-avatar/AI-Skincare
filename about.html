The Beginning

Why we built Senevi

Senevi started with a simple frustration: skincare shelves are crowded, and most advice online is really just an advertisement in disguise. Our founders — a dermatology researcher and a machine learning engineer — wanted a way to answer one honest question: what does this specific skin actually need?

The name Senevi comes from an old word for "loving-kindness" — the same care a trusted friend gives when they tell you the truth about what works, not what's popular. That care is baked into every recommendation we make.

Our Mission

Personalized skincare, without the guesswork.

To help every person discover the most suitable skincare products using artificial intelligence — making beauty shopping easier, more personalized, and more trustworthy, regardless of which brand makes the product.

Our Vision

A world where beauty advice is earned, not bought.

We imagine a future where AI-guided skincare is the default — where anyone, anywhere, can get dermatology-informed, unbiased product guidance in under a minute.
